I teach academic literacy and lead English language programs for international students.

I have worked in U.S. and international higher education for more than ten years, including six years at universities in Japan.

I have managed international student programs across the full program lifecycle: academic and cultural programming, budgets, staffing, student support, partnerships, logistics, assessment, and continuous improvement.
